# 📊 Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **supply chain data** to understand product sales, revenue, inventory, shipping costs, lead time, transportation methods, and product performance.

The dashboard was created to identify important trends and patterns in supply chain operations and provide business insights that can support better operational and strategic decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Supply chain businesses need to monitor sales, inventory, transportation, shipping costs, and delivery performance to ensure efficient operations.

This project aims to provide a clear view of supply chain performance and identify areas that may require improvement.

### **Key Business Questions**

* What is the overall supply chain performance?
* Which product types generate the highest revenue?
* Which product types have the highest production volume?
* Which locations generate the highest revenue and product sales?
* What are the average shipping costs and lead times?
* Which transportation modes are used most frequently?
* Which product types have the highest shipping times?
* Which routes and product types perform best?
* What actions can improve supply chain efficiency?

## 🔑 Key Insights

### **Insight 1 – Skincare is the strongest product type**

Skincare generates the highest revenue among the displayed product types, with approximately **242K**, compared with around **174K for Haircare** and **162K for Cosmetics**.

Skincare also has the highest production volume, at approximately **24.4K units**.

### **Insight 2 – Mumbai and Kolkata are important locations**

Mumbai generates approximately **138K in revenue**, making it the highest-revenue location shown.

Kolkata follows closely with approximately **137K**, while Chennai generates around **119K**.

This indicates that these locations are important contributors to overall supply chain performance.

### **Insight 3 – Road and Rail are major transportation modes**

Road transportation represents approximately **29%** of the transportation distribution, while Rail represents around **28%**.

Air accounts for approximately **26%**, and Sea represents around **17%**.

This suggests that road and rail transportation play an important role in the current supply chain network.

### **Insight 4 – Skincare has the highest shipping time**

Among the displayed product types, Skincare has the highest shipping time at approximately **213**, followed by Haircare at **191** and Cosmetics at **171**.

This could indicate an opportunity to investigate the logistics process for Skincare products.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

1. **Focus on high-performing product categories.**
   The business should maintain sufficient inventory and production capacity for Skincare because it generates the highest revenue and production volume.

2. **Improve logistics for Skincare products.**
   Since Skincare has the highest shipping time, the company should investigate transportation routes, warehouse locations, and delivery processes to reduce delays.

3. **Strengthen operations in high-performing locations.**
   Locations such as Mumbai and Kolkata generate high revenue, so the business could prioritize inventory availability and distribution capacity in these markets.

4. **Review transportation efficiency.**
   Since Road and Rail account for a large share of transportation, the company should compare their cost, speed, and reliability to identify the most efficient transportation strategy.

5. **Monitor inventory and stock levels.**
   Regular monitoring of stock levels can help reduce both stockouts and excess inventory, especially for high-demand product types.
